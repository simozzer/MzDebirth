# MzDebirth: your first half hour

A hands-on tour, from the demo song to a song of your own, about ten minutes a part. The
[README](../README.md) is the full manual; this is the order to meet things in. If something
doesn't do what you expect, see [Troubleshooting](../README.md#troubleshooting).

## The one thing to know first: SONG MODE

MzDebirth plays the machines in one of two ways, and the green **SONG MODE** light, at the
right of the row with the RACK and ARRANGE tabs, says which:

- **SONG MODE on** - the *song* in the Arrange window plays the machines, bar by bar, the
  way a sequencer plays a track. Picking patterns on the machines doesn't change what PLAY
  plays: the song does.
- **SONG MODE off** - like ReBirth's PATTERN mode: each machine plays the pattern picked on
  its own pads, round and round, and you play the rack live.

A new rack starts with SONG MODE **on**, playing the demo song. That's why the pattern pads
can seem to do nothing at first.

## 1. Hear the demo song

1. Press **PLAY** (or the space bar). The demo song plays: two minutes of acid rave, with an
   intro, builds, drops and a breakdown.
2. Click **ARRANGE** (or press F2) to watch it go by, a track for each machine. The lines over
   the parts are knobs turning: the filters opening, a snare roll getting louder.
3. Click **RACK** (or F1) to see the machines. The knobs the song moves turn by themselves.
4. Press **STOP**.

## 2. Play the rack live

1. Click **SONG MODE** so its light goes **off**.
2. Press **PLAY**. Every machine plays its pattern **A1**: the acid house groove.
3. On any machine's **PATTERN** pads, pick another number, say **2**. It blinks, and comes in
   when the pattern playing comes round to its start, so the change is always in time.
4. Pick a different **bank** - **A** acid house, **B** techno, **C** electro, **D** deep house -
   and a number. Pattern *n* of every machine is written to play with pattern *n* of the
   others, so picking the same number everywhere always works. In each bank 1-3, 6 and 7 are
   grooves, 4 and 8 fills, and 5 a breakdown.
5. Turn some knobs: the 303's **CUT OFF**, **RESO** and **ENV MOD** are the acid sound. Try the
   **PCF** and **DELAY** units along the top, and each machine's mixer strip on the right:
   fader, pan, the **DELAY** and **REVERB** sends, **MUTE**.
6. Press a machine's **STOP** to drop it out at the next bar; pick a pattern to bring it back.
   That is most of what playing a ReBirth live is.

## 3. Write a pattern

1. Stop, then press **NEW** and choose **Empty rack**. (NEW and **Acid demo** bring the demo
   back whenever you want it - or try **Ambient demo**, the sea and whales.)
2. With SONG MODE off, press **PLAY**. Silence: every pattern is empty.
3. **Drums** - on the Mz808, pick an instrument on the select row (**BD**, the bass drum) and
   click steps **1, 5, 9 and 13** on the sixteen keys. Pick **CH**, the closed hat, and click
   every other step. **AC** sets accents.
4. **Bass** - on the Mz303, click into the grid under the knobs: a click is a note, another
   click is a rest, and you can drag along. The rows under the notes are **UP** and **DOWN**
   (an octave), **ACCENT** and **SLIDE**. If the grid is hidden, click the **STEP SEQUENCER**
   bar over it to unfold it.
5. **STEPS** sets how long a pattern is; **COPY** and **PASTE** copy it to another pad to vary.
6. **GENERATE**, in the KEY section at the top, writes eight new patterns into a bank, in
   the **KEY** and **STYLE** shown, for when you'd rather start from something.

## 4. Make a song

The quickest way is to play it, as ReBirth recorded:

1. Click **ARRANGE**, and click the ruler at bar 1 to put the cursor there.
2. Press **REC** in the top transport (it blinks), then **PLAY**.
3. Play the rack as in part 2: pick patterns, stop and start machines, turn knobs. Everything
   you play appears in the Arrange window as you go, knob moves included.
4. Press **STOP**. Turn **SONG MODE** on, put the cursor back at bar 1, and press **PLAY**:
   the song plays back what you did.

Not right? **Ctrl+Z** undoes the whole take. You can also **overdub** - with the song
playing, press REC and pick patterns over it - or edit the parts by hand: drag, copy, cut
and glue them, or double-click one to edit its notes. See
[Recording a song](../README.md#recording-a-song) and
[The Arrange window](../README.md#the-arrange-window).

## 5. Keep it

- **SAVE** keeps the whole rack - patterns, knobs, effects and the song - as a `.mzdebirth`
  file; **LOAD** opens one.
- **EXPORT**, in the Arrange window, renders the song to a WAV or FLAC file to play anywhere.
