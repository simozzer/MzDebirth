A second way to work: beside the ReBirth-style rack, an **Arrange window** after Cubase on
the Atari ST, to lay the patterns out as a song and write parts of any length.

## Downloads

- **MzDebirth-0.4.0-win64-Setup.exe** - the installer: the app, a Start menu entry, an
  uninstaller, and the Mz950's six sample disks. Per-user installs need no admin rights.
- **MzDebirth-0.4.0-win64.zip** - the same, to run without installing. Keep the `disks`
  folder beside `MzDebirth.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The app is not code-signed, so Windows may warn about an unknown
publisher: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's new

- **A demo song** to open with: an acid rave tune made from the factory grooves, two minutes
  at 132 bpm with an intro, two builds - snare rolls speeding up, a riser climbing, a beat of
  silence - two drops and a breakdown. Press PLAY. **NEW** brings it back.
- **The Arrange window** (the ARRANGE tab, or F2): tracks down the left, each playing one of
  the machines - as many as you like - and on them parts, along a ruler of bars with left
  and right locators and a cycle.
  - **Cubase's toolbox**, along the top and on the right mouse button: ARROW, PENCIL, ERASER,
    SCISSORS, GLUE and MUTE, with SNAP to a bar, a beat or a sixteenth.
  - **The ST's keypad keys**: Enter plays, 0 stops, `.` goes back to the start, `/` turns the
    cycle on and off. Undo and redo for every edit.
  - **Insert a pattern** from the rack onto a track, and it plays exactly as the pattern did -
    slides, accents, ties and shuffle and all - so the factory grooves, GENERATE and TRANSPOSE
    all feed the song.
- **KEY EDIT** for the 303, 101 and 950, and **DRUM EDIT** for the 808 and 909 - double-click
  a part. Notes of any length at any position, a velocity lane, and quantize. On the 303 and
  101, overlapping notes slide.
- **SONG MODE**: the song plays the machines, or - off, as ReBirth's PATTERN mode - their
  patterns. Changed while playing, it waits for the bar. Every knob, fader and effect plays
  the song as it plays the patterns.
- **The PCF is MzPCF's** - the same filter, envelope and 32 patterns as the
  [MzPCF](https://github.com/simozzer/MzPCF) plugin, compiled from its repository. Each strike
  now opens with a third of a millisecond's rounding, so it does not click, and its knobs move
  smoothly.
- Songs are saved in `.mzdebirth` files with the rest of the rack. Racks saved by 0.3 open
  with an empty song.
