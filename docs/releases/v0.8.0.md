A reverb for the rack, and a link back to where the next version will be.

## Downloads

- **MzDebirth-0.8.0-win64-Setup.exe** - Windows: the installer. The app, a Start menu entry,
  an uninstaller, and the Mz950's seven sample disks. Per-user installs need no admin rights.
- **MzDebirth-0.8.0-win64.zip** - Windows: the same, to run without installing. Keep the
  `disks` folder beside `MzDebirth.exe`.
- **MzDebirth-0.8.0-macOS-beta.zip** - macOS (beta): the app and the Mz950's disks, with an
  install script.

**Windows:** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The app is not code-signed, so Windows may warn about
an unknown publisher: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**macOS:** Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or later. The download isn't signed by
Apple, which takes a paid developer account, so macOS won't open it with a double-click.
Unzip it, then in Terminal type `bash ` (with a space), drag `install.sh` from the unzipped
folder into the window, and press Return. It puts the app in Applications and the disks in
`~/Library/Application Support/MzDebirth`, and clears the quarantine flag. `READ ME FIRST.txt`
in the zip explains how to install by hand instead.

## What's new

- **REVERB**, a fifth effects unit, between DELAY and COMP:
  - **DECAY** from a small room to a long hall, **TONE** from dark (the highs dying first)
    to bright, and up to 150 ms of **PRE**-delay so the sound itself comes through first.
  - It's a send, like the DELAY: every machine's mixer strip has a **REVERB** knob, under
    PAN and DELAY - or **REV** beside them on a folded machine. They start at 0, so nothing
    changes until you turn one up.
  - It comes back wide from a mono send, and rings on after STOP.
  - Racks saved by 0.7 open with every REVERB send at 0.
- **A link to the project page**, between the RACK and ARRANGE tabs and SONG MODE: the
  version you have, and github.com/simozzer/MzDebirth, where the next one, its notes and the
  issue tracker are. Click it to open the page in your browser. MzDebirth never goes online
  by itself.

Everything else is as in [0.7.0](https://github.com/simozzer/MzDebirth/releases/tag/v0.7.0).
