A piano for the Mz950: the sample disks that come with MzDebirth now include **PIANO.hfe**.

## Downloads

- **MzDebirth-0.4.1-win64-Setup.exe** - the installer: the app, a Start menu entry, an
  uninstaller, and the Mz950's seven sample disks. Per-user installs need no admin rights.
- **MzDebirth-0.4.1-win64.zip** - the same, to run without installing. Keep the `disks`
  folder beside `MzDebirth.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The app is not code-signed, so Windows may warn about an unknown
publisher: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's new

- **PIANO**, a seventh disk for the Mz950, from [Mz950 0.6.2](https://github.com/simozzer/Mz950/releases/tag/v0.6.2).
  It's a grand piano in the style of the factory multisamples: nine samples from E1 to G5,
  synthesised from a struck string's partials rather than recorded. It has four programmes:
  - **GRAND PNO**: the piano.
  - **BRIGHT PNO**: brighter, for a band.
  - **MELLOW PNO**: rounder, with a longer release.
  - **HONKYTONK**: two detuned layers, four notes at a time.

  Open it on the Mz950 with **LOAD DISK**. It's in the `disks` folder with the others.

Everything else is as in [0.4.0](https://github.com/simozzer/MzDebirth/releases/tag/v0.4.0).
