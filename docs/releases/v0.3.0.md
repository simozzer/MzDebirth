A rack of the Mz synths on one step sequencer, in the spirit of ReBirth RB-338: the
**Mz303**, **Mz101**, **Mz808**, **Mz909** and **Mz950**, each with its full panel, its own
patterns and a channel on the mixer, all on one clock.

## Downloads

- **MzDebirth-0.3.0-win64-Setup.exe** - the installer: the app, a Start menu entry, an
  uninstaller, and the Mz950's six sample disks. Per-user installs need no admin rights.
- **MzDebirth-0.3.0-win64.zip** - the same, to run without installing. Keep the `disks`
  folder beside `MzDebirth.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The app is not code-signed, so Windows may warn about an unknown
publisher: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's in it

- **Five machines**, each with the synth's real engine: the 303 and 101 written on a piano
  roll with UP, DOWN, ACCENT and SLIDE; the 808 and 909 the hardware's way, an instrument
  and its sixteen step keys; the 950 playing S900/S950 disk images, any number of notes on
  a step, with ties.
- **Patterns** - four banks of eight per machine, sixteen steps each; a pattern picked while
  playing waits for the one playing to come round.
- **Factory grooves** in every bank: acid house, techno, electro and deep house, pattern *n*
  of each machine written to play with pattern *n* of the others. **NEW** brings them back,
  or clears the rack.
- **Effects**, as ReBirth had them: **DIST**, **PCF** (a filter played by 32 step patterns),
  a tempo-synced **DELAY**, and **COMP** - per channel and on the whole mix.
- **Keys for harmonic mixing** - set the key as Mixed In Key names it on the Camelot wheel
  (1A-12B) and see what mixes with it; **GENERATE** a bank of new grooves in that key and a
  style, or **TRANSPOSE** a bank into it.
- **Transport and mixer** - tempo, shuffle per machine, level, pan, mute and the effects
  sends; racks saved to and loaded from `.mzdebirth` files.
