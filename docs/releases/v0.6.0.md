The song and the rack as one: a track for each machine, a way from each track to its
machine, step sequencers that fold away, a meter on every channel, and a window that grows
taller.

## Downloads

- **MzDebirth-0.6.0-win64-Setup.exe** - the installer: the app, a Start menu entry, an
  uninstaller, and the Mz950's seven sample disks. Per-user installs need no admin rights.
- **MzDebirth-0.6.0-win64.zip** - the same, to run without installing. Keep the `disks`
  folder beside `MzDebirth.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The app is not code-signed, so Windows may warn about an unknown
publisher: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's new

- **A track for each machine.** The Arrange window always has five tracks, one per machine
  in the rack and in the rack's order, so the song and the rack always match. **+ TRACK**
  has gone, along with deleting, moving and re-assigning tracks; a track can still be
  renamed and recoloured.
  - A song saved by 0.5 or earlier with two tracks for one machine opens with them merged
    into one, and sounds as it did: a part from a track that was muted on its own comes
    over muted.
  - The demo song now has five tracks. The 909's grooves and rolls share a track, as do the
    101's lead and its risers and pad, which take turns.
- **RACK on every track** goes straight to that machine in the rack, scrolled into view and
  ringed for a moment, so you can turn its knobs. The track's right-click menu does the
  same.
- **Fold the step sequencers away.** The Mz303, Mz101 and Mz950 have a **STEP SEQUENCER**
  bar under their knobs. Click it and the machine folds down to its knobs, with its pattern
  section and mixer strip packed down beside them. The bar stays where it was, to unfold it
  again, and the rack remembers which are folded. A folded 303 is about a third of its
  height, a folded 950 under half.
- **A meter on every channel**, beside the fader: a stereo peak meter, green to -12 dB,
  yellow to 0 and red over it, with the highest point held for a second. It reads what the
  machine puts into the output - after its fader, pan and the MASTER - so red means that
  machine alone would take the output past full scale.
- **A taller window.** Dragging the window wider still scales everything; dragging it taller
  now shows more of the rack, or of the song, at the same size. It can't go below 800
  pixels tall, and it opens at the size it was left.

Racks saved by 0.5 open as before.
