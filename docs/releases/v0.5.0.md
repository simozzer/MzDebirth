For playing the rack alongside records: a tempo to a tenth of a beat, and **DRAG** and
**PUSH** buttons to nudge it into phase, as on a CD deck.

## Downloads

- **MzDebirth-0.5.0-win64-Setup.exe** - the installer: the app, a Start menu entry, an
  uninstaller, and the Mz950's seven sample disks. Per-user installs need no admin rights.
- **MzDebirth-0.5.0-win64.zip** - the same, to run without installing. Keep the `disks`
  folder beside `MzDebirth.exe`.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The app is not code-signed, so Windows may warn about an unknown
publisher: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's new

- **TEMPO to a tenth**: the display reads `128.4`, on the rack and in the Arrange window.
  Drag it or use the wheel for whole beats a minute; hold **Shift** for tenths. Double-click
  still sets 120. Racks are saved with the tempo to a tenth, and older racks open as before.
- **DRAG ◀ and PUSH ▶**, beside the tempo, work like the pitch-bend buttons on a CD deck:
  - Held, they slow the rack or hurry it, by 3% straight away and building to 10% over two
    seconds.
  - Let go, and the rack is back on its tempo, with the beat moved by however much the nudge
    gained or lost. That's how the rack is pulled into phase with a record.
  - While one is held, its light is on and the tempo shows the bent figure.
- **The left and right arrow keys** DRAG and PUSH while held, on the rack and in the Arrange
  window.

Everything else is as in [0.4.1](https://github.com/simozzer/MzDebirth/releases/tag/v0.4.1).
