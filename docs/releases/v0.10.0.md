A limiter and a meter on the master, and songs exported to WAV or FLAC.

## Downloads

- **MzDebirth-0.10.0-win64-Setup.exe** - Windows: the installer. The app, a Start menu entry,
  an uninstaller, and the Mz950's seven sample disks. Per-user installs need no admin rights.
- **MzDebirth-0.10.0-win64.zip** - Windows: the same, to run without installing. Keep the
  `disks` folder beside `MzDebirth.exe`.
- **MzDebirth-0.10.0-macOS-beta.zip** - macOS (beta): the app and the Mz950's disks, with an
  install script.

**Windows:** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The app is not code-signed, so Windows may warn about
an unknown publisher: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**macOS:** Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or later. The download isn't signed by
Apple, which takes a paid developer account, so macOS won't open it with a double-click.
Unzip it, then in Terminal type `bash ` (with a space), drag `install.sh` from the unzipped
folder into the window, and press Return. It puts the app in Applications and the disks in
`~/Library/Application Support/MzDebirth`, and clears the quarantine flag. `READ ME FIRST.txt`
in the zip explains how to install by hand instead.

## What's new

- **LIMIT**, a limiter on the whole mix and the last thing before the output, so the rack
  never clips, however hard it's pushed. It holds every peak under -0.3 dBFS, looking 1.5 ms
  ahead so it's ready for each one, and leaves the sound untouched when it isn't needed. It's
  on to start with, racks saved before included. Its light is green while it waits and
  orange while it's working; if it's orange much of the time, turn the MASTER down a little.
- **OUT**, a meter on the master, beside the RACK and ARRANGE tabs: what the rack puts out,
  left and right, after the MASTER and LIMIT.
- **EXPORT**, in the Arrange window's toolbar: the song to a **WAV** or **FLAC** file, 24-bit at
  48 kHz. It renders the whole song, or between the locators when CYCLE is on, with four
  seconds more for the delay and reverb to ring out. It runs on a copy of the rack, much
  faster than real time, so you can keep playing while it works. For an MP3, convert the WAV.

Everything else is as in [0.9.0](https://github.com/simozzer/MzDebirth/releases/tag/v0.9.0).
