Record a song by playing the rack, as ReBirth did: takes, overdubs and knob automation.

## Downloads

- **MzDebirth-0.9.0-win64-Setup.exe** - Windows: the installer. The app, a Start menu entry,
  an uninstaller, and the Mz950's seven sample disks. Per-user installs need no admin rights.
- **MzDebirth-0.9.0-win64.zip** - Windows: the same, to run without installing. Keep the
  `disks` folder beside `MzDebirth.exe`.
- **MzDebirth-0.9.0-macOS-beta.zip** - macOS (beta): the app and the Mz950's disks, with an
  install script.

**Windows:** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The app is not code-signed, so Windows may warn about
an unknown publisher: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**macOS:** Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or later. The download isn't signed by
Apple, which takes a paid developer account, so macOS won't open it with a double-click.
Unzip it, then in Terminal type `bash ` (with a space), drag `install.sh` from the unzipped
folder into the window, and press Return. It puts the app in Applications and the disks in
`~/Library/Application Support/MzDebirth`, and clears the quarantine flag. `READ ME FIRST.txt`
in the zip explains how to install by hand instead.

## What's new

- **Recording a song.** Put the Arrange window's cursor where the take should start, press
  **REC** in the transport (it blinks), then **PLAY**. Every machine plays its patterns, and
  each stretch of one pattern goes into the song as a part named after it, appearing as you
  play. **STOP** ends the take; **REC** again ends it and keeps playing. One Ctrl+Z takes it
  all back.
- **Overdubbing.** With the song playing, press **REC**, then pick a pattern on any machine:
  it takes that machine over at the next bar and is recorded over its track from there.
  **REC** again hands every machine back to the song at the next bar, and the song carries on
  as it was.
- **Automation.** While a take or an overdub records, every knob and switch you move is
  recorded too - each machine's panel, its mixer strip, and the effects units. When the song
  plays, the knobs turn by themselves; take hold of one and the song leaves it to you. The
  Arrange window draws each track's automation over its lane, with an **A** by its name and
  an **AUTOMATION** selector to show all of it, none, or one control's. The effects units'
  automation is on a new **FX** row. Right-click a track, or FX, to clear it.
- **Patterns over the song.** While the song plays, a pattern picked on a machine takes it
  over at the next bar; click the lit pattern again to hand it back.
- **STOP on each machine.** Pressed while playing, the machine stops at the start of the next
  bar; pick a pattern to bring it back in. It works in SONG mode too.
- **Parts that loop.** Drag a part's right edge longer and it repeats to fill the length, as
  a clip does in Ableton Live; drag it shorter and out again and it still loops.
- **The demo song** has a long snare roll building through the breakdown into the second
  drop, and automation: the rolls brought up, the filters opening through the builds, and the
  reverb up for the breakdown. NEW, with the factory grooves, brings it in.
- **Smaller things:**
  - The song position shows beside SONG MODE in the rack.
  - The Arrange window's tracks are taller.
  - The step editors show the pattern you pick while stopped, and after PASTE, CLEAR or
    STEPS.

Songs saved by 0.8 open as they were. A song saved by 0.9 opens in 0.8 without its
automation.

Everything else is as in [0.8.0](https://github.com/simozzer/MzDebirth/releases/tag/v0.8.0).
